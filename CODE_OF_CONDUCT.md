# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Standards

Use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback,
focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy. Harassment, trolling,
personal attacks, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and other
unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct privately to the project maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Maintainers will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy, and may correct, warn,
temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants based on impact.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
